COMMUNITY OUTREACH

Widows Care

Widows Care is a community outreach initiative that demonstrates love, compassion, and practical support for elderly women.

On designated community service days, including Charity Friday, children and staff from Susana Homes visit women's homes to assist with everyday household tasks.

These activities may include fetching water, gathering firewood, weeding, and washing clothes. Through these visits, the children learn the importance of kindness, service, and caring for others.

Outreach Coordinator: Mrs. Salome Ohaja

COMMUNITY OUTREACH

Tea Time Stories

Tea Time Stories is an outreach programme that provides an opportunity to feed elderly women physically and spiritually. Twice a month, the outreach team visits elderly women to read scriptures with them.

Tea and biscuits are shared, creating an opportunity for fellowship and for people to hear the Word of God in their local language.

Frequency: Twice a month

Monthly support: $2 per woman

Current recipients: 10+

Fundraising goal: $240

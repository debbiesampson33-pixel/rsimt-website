Susana Homes

A place of care, support and hope within the Right Steps family.

OUR PURPOSE

Supporting People and Communities

Susana Homes is a place where children are cared for, supported and encouraged to grow in a safe and loving environment.

“Whoever welcomes one such child in my name welcomes me.” — Matthew 18:5

Care

Providing a caring environment for the children.

Support

Supporting children and communities through appropriate programmes.

Education

Creating opportunities for learning, development and personal growth.

Community

Encouraging kindness, service and positive community involvement.

OUR CAMPUSES

Susana Homes Campuses

Susana Homes has two campuses, each providing care, community and opportunities for the children.

Susana Homes Campus One

Campus One is home to children who grow together in a caring and supportive community.

The children gather to worship God through the Church of Christ @ Susana Home .

Susana Homes Campus Two

Campus Two provides another place of care, community and development for the children of Susana Homes.

The children gather to worship God through the Church of Christ Oyigbo .

CHURCH OF CHRIST, UMUAHALA

Children's Care & Bible Teaching

Right Steps Incorporated supports these programmes to help provide food and nutritional support for children while encouraging their spiritual growth.

Every Sunday

Vitamin Church

Vitamin Church is a children's programme held every Sunday at the Church of Christ, Umuahala. The programme provides food and nutritional support for children as an expression of love, care, and concern for their well-being.

The children receive:

  • Rice
  • Eggs
  • Beans
  • Milk
  • Vitamins

Supported by Right Steps Incorporated.

Every Thursday

Feed My Flock

Feed My Flock is a children's programme held every Thursday at the Church of Christ, Umuahala. The programme provides food and nutritional support while teaching the children the Word of God.

The children receive:

  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Vitamins

During the programme, the children are also taught biblical lessons to help them learn about God and grow in their faith.

Supported by Right Steps Incorporated.

Vitamin Church and Feed My Flock focus on the care and spiritual development of children in the Umuahala community. Both programmes are supported by Right Steps Incorporated.

ABOUT OUR BOARDING PROGRAMME

Learning, Growth & Community

The RSCS boarding programme is designed to provide students with a supportive school environment that encourages academic growth, discipline, responsibility and good character.

📚

Academic Support

Dedicated study periods and a structured environment that encourages students to focus on their academic work.

🏠

Boarding Community

Students experience a community that encourages responsibility, cooperation and respect for others.

🙏

Christian Values

Students are encouraged to develop good character and grow in Christian values.

OUR FOUNDER

A Vision Built on Purpose

Right Steps Incorporated was established with a vision of creating opportunities that positively influence individuals, families and communities.

Through education, practical skills, Christian values and community service, the organization seeks to encourage people to discover their abilities and use them meaningfully.

This vision has inspired the development of educational institutions and other initiatives under Right Steps Incorporated, providing opportunities for learning, personal development and service.

The founder's vision continues to guide the organization as it works toward building responsible, skilled and purpose-driven individuals.

THE VISION

Building People. Building Communities.

The work of Right Steps Incorporated is centered on education, development, service and positive impact.

Education

Creating opportunities for people to learn and develop their potential.

Skills

Encouraging practical skills and meaningful Work-to-Learn opportunities.

Faith

Promoting Christian values, character and responsible leadership.

Service

Encouraging people to contribute positively to their communities.

RIGHT STEPS
